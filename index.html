02 Studio Philosophy & Practice

We design interiors with soul — where Indian craft, natural material and considered colour make a home feel unmistakably its own.

The Mystic Canvas is an Indore-based studio devoted to warm, characterful interiors. We layer teak, cane, terracotta jaali and handmade tile with rich, lived-in colour — never decoration for its own sake.

Every project begins with listening — to the light a room receives, the way a family moves through it, and the stories it is meant to hold. Only then do we begin to draw.

A

Residential Sanctuaries

Full homes and apartments designed as warm, personal retreats — every threshold, niche and surface considered.

B

Bespoke Bedrooms

Custom headboards, wardrobes and dressing spaces — tailored joinery in teak, cane and quiet colour.

C

Artisan Detailing

Jaali screens, carved doors, pooja niches and tile work — the handcrafted details that give a space its signature.
